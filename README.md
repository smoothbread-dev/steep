# Steep 🍵

A quiet little app for couples who want to genuinely understand each other better.

Steep generates warm, honest conversation questions — the kind you never thought to ask the person you know best. Not therapy. Not a quiz. Just two people, a good question, and a real conversation.

---

## What it does

- Generates one question at a time, built to spark real conversation between two people
- Questions get deeper as the night goes on — starting light, ending honest
- Tracks what you've already talked about so nothing repeats
- Mood-aware — respects whether tonight is light and easy or something heavier

---

## How it works

A prompt tells the AI the one rule that matters:

> *It's not about the topic — it's about how you ask.*

Any question can land well if it's wrapped in enough warmth and context. Steep makes sure every question feels like it came from a person, not an app.

---
